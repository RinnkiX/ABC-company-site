In early 2025 we tested a four-day week for our whole team. A year on, it stays. The shorter week forced us to cut low-value meetings and protect deep work. Client deadlines still hit, and retention improved noticeably. The honest part: we plan more carefully and occasionally work a fifth day before a launch. The trade-off has been worth it — better work, calmer people, and no drop in output.
