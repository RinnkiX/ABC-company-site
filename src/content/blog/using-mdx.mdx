A first call with an agency shouldn't feel like an interview. Come with your goal and a budget range, and leave with a sense of whether they listen. Good questions to ask: who does the actual work, how often you'll see progress, and what happens if a deadline slips. Red flags are vague answers and pressure to sign quickly. The right partner talks about your business, not their portfolio.
