We're often told bigger means better, but in web projects the opposite can be true. A small team means fewer handoffs, shorter meetings, and a designer and developer who talk every day. Clients speak directly to the people doing the work instead of a middle layer. When something changes mid-project, it can be adapted the same afternoon. For most businesses, the biggest risk isn't a small team. It's a team that's too far from the work.
