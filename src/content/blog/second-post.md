A regional clinic came to us with a simple problem: patients kept booking by phone, but fewer were finding them online. We rebuilt the site around clear service pages, added same-day booking, and cut the homepage copy in half. Within three months, online appointments doubled and the front desk finally stopped fielding repeat questions. The work was small in scope, but the changes were felt across the whole practice.
