Every project follows the same path. It starts with a short call to understand the goal, followed by a written proposal with clear scope and pricing. Once signed, we build in public — sharing progress every week rather than disappearing for months. Review rounds are scheduled, so feedback arrives when there's still time to act. The final step is a handover: passwords, training, and a short walkthrough. Nothing surprising, nothing skipped.
