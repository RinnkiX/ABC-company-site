It happens gradually. Traffic holds steady, but enquiries slow down. The design starts to feel dated on mobile. Pages load slowly, and the booking form keeps dropping visitors. If any of these ring true, a redesign can be a smarter investment than more advertising. Start with a content audit, cut what isn't working, and rebuild around the three actions you want visitors to take. A focused site usually pays for itself within a year.
